## Signature drinks

### Golden Hour Latte
*Saffron, honey and the last warm light of the day.*

A silky latte sweetened with saffron-steeped honey and finished with pistachio dust. Floral, gently sweet, deeply golden.

| | |
|---|---|
| Serving temperature | 62–65 °C |
| Cup size | 12 oz / 355 ml ceramic |
| Presentation | Warm stoneware cup on an oat-coloured saucer with a single pistachio shortbread. |
| Garnish | Saffron threads, pistachio dust |
| Est. ingredient cost | $1.28 |
| Suggested price | $6.80 |
| Est. ingredient margin | 81.2% |
| Caffeine | yes |
| Target customer | Flavour-curious professionals and gifting customers who want a drink that photographs as well as it tastes. |
| Story | Named for the thirty minutes when a café feels most like itself — light slanting low, everyone slowing down. |

**Ingredients**
- Double espresso (18 g in / 36 g out) — 36 ml
- Saffron-honey syrup* — 15 ml
- Whole milk (or oat) — 200 ml
- Crushed pistachio — 1 g
- Saffron threads (garnish) — 3 threads

**Method**
1. *Syrup: steep 0.4 g saffron in 50 g hot water 15 min, stir into 100 g honey. Keep chilled up to 2 weeks.
2. Pull the double espresso directly onto the syrup in a pre-warmed 12 oz cup and stir.
3. Steam milk to 60–65 °C with fine microfoam; pour in a tight rosetta.
4. Dust with pistachio and float three saffron threads.

### First Light
*Espresso over yuzu tonic — a sparkling sunrise.*

Bright, citrusy and bubbly: chilled espresso poured over yuzu-honey tonic so the drink shifts from amber to gold as you sip.

| | |
|---|---|
| Serving temperature | Served 2–4 °C |
| Cup size | 16 oz / 473 ml tall glass |
| Presentation | Tall Collins glass with a paper straw and a visible amber-to-gold gradient. |
| Garnish | Dehydrated lemon wheel, thyme |
| Est. ingredient cost | $1.50 |
| Suggested price | $6.40 |
| Est. ingredient margin | 76.6% |
| Caffeine | yes |
| Target customer | Summer and post-gym customers who want caffeine without milk. |
| Story | The moment before the city wakes: cold, clear and full of promise. |

**Ingredients**
- Espresso, shot (18 g in / 36 g out) — 36 ml
- Tonic water — 120 ml
- Yuzu juice — 15 ml
- Honey syrup (1:1) — 10 ml
- Ice — 150 g
- Dehydrated lemon wheel + thyme — 1 + 1 sprig

**Method**
1. Fill a tall glass with ice; add yuzu juice and honey syrup, stir briefly.
2. Top with tonic water, pouring down a spoon to keep carbonation.
3. Pull the espresso and pour slowly over the back of a spoon so it floats as a layer.
4. Garnish with dehydrated lemon and thyme. Serve unstirred.

### Dusk Ember
*Date syrup, torched cinnamon and a pinch of sea salt.*

A deep, caramel-toned latte sweetened with slow-cooked Medjool dates and perfumed by a torched cinnamon stick.

| | |
|---|---|
| Serving temperature | 62 °C |

| Est. ingredient cost | $1.15 |
| Suggested price | $6.60 |
| Est. ingredient margin | 82.6% |
| Caffeine | yes |
| Target customer | Evening customers and anyone who loves toffee and spice without refined sugar. |
| Story | Inspired by the smell of a fire dying down — warm, smoky, reluctant to end. |

**Ingredients**
- Double espresso — 36 ml
- Medjool date syrup* — 20 ml
- Whole milk — 180 ml
- Cinnamon stick (torched) — 1 small
- Flaked sea salt — 1 pinch

**Method**
1. *Syrup: simmer 100 g pitted Medjool dates with 200 ml water 15 min, blend and strain.
2. Combine espresso and date syrup in a warm cup.
3. Steam milk to 62 °C and pour; finish with salt.
4. Torch the cinnamon stick, rest it on the saucer and cloche briefly for aroma at the table.
