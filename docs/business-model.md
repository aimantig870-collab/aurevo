# Aurevo — business model & preliminary financial model

> **Every figure is an ESTIMATE built from stated assumptions, not quotations or market data.** Replace with supplier quotes, landlord terms, local labour rates and legal advice before relying on any number. Nothing here is an earnings claim or investment advice.

## 1. Revenue models explored
| Model | How it earns | Requirements | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Own café (pilot) | Direct sales | Site, fit-out, permits, team | Prerequisite for everything else |
| Coffee-shop partnerships | Co-branded sections; supply of syrups/beans; menu fee | Tested recipes, supply chain, partner agreements | After pilot |
| Signature beverage licensing | Annual licence per venue + per-drink royalty | Trademark, recipe IP protection, QA audits | After trademark + testing |
| Branded product collaborations | Revenue share / wholesale | Food-safety certs, packaging dielines | Post-pilot |
| Brand licensing | Fee for use of name/identity | Registered marks, brand rules | Post-trademark |
| Franchise partnerships | Initial fee + royalty + marketing fund | Disclosure docs, validated unit economics, legal per country | Only after readiness gates |
| Digital brand consulting | Project fees | Case studies from the pilot | Optional |

## 3. Recurring costs
Ingredients (~22%) + packaging (~5%) · labour · rent · utilities · software/POS · insurance · marketing · repairs · card fees · waste.

## 4. Annual operating scenarios (single pilot café)
Assumptions: average ticket **$9.50**, 360 trading days, COGS **27%** of revenue (ingredients+packaging), rent **$84k** fixed, other opex **$60k + 3% of revenue**, labour stepped by volume. *Transactions/day are scenarios, not forecasts.*

| | Low | Base | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transactions/day | 120 | 200 | 280 |
| Revenue | $410,400 | $684,000 | $957,600 |
| COGS (27%) | $110,808 | $184,680 | $258,552 |
| Labour | $200,000 | $230,000 | $270,000 |
| Rent | $84,000 | $84,000 | $84,000 |
| Other opex | $72,312 | $80,520 | $88,728 |
| **EBITDA** | **–$56,720 (–13.8%)** | **$104,800 (15.3%)** | **$256,320 (26.8%)** |

Reading it honestly: the low case loses money; the base case would take roughly 2–4 years to recover the investment *before* ramp-up, tax, financing and owner pay; the high case is optimistic. Real cafés often ramp slowly in year 1.

## 5. Franchisor economics (illustrative placeholders — fees to be set by counsel/market study)
If a royalty of 5% applied to base-case unit revenue → **$34,200 per unit per year**. With franchisor overhead of $300k/yr (team, QA, training, marketing), royalties alone cover overhead at ≈ **9 operating units**. Initial fees and supply margin would add to this but are not modelled. *These percentages are placeholders, not benchmarked.*

## 6. Partnership structure (proposed)
Memorandum of understanding → pilot period with QA audits → licence/franchise agreement drafted by local counsel. Brand-use rules, recipe confidentiality, supplier standards, audit rights and termination terms to be defined.

## 7. Licensing & operational requirements
Trademark registration · recipe/IP protection · food-safety certification (HACCP or local equivalent) · allergen labelling · business licences/permits · insurance · supplier agreements · staff training standards.

## 8. Risks
Name conflict · unproven recipes and costs · specialty-ingredient supply/shelf-life · concentration on a single pilot · lease risk · labour costs · franchise regulation (heavily regulated in many countries) · brand dilution from partners · over-claiming (reputational/legal).

## 9. Expansion opportunities
Kiosks and shop-in-shop formats · airport/hotel concessions · packaged cold brew and syrups · seasonal collabs · corporate gifting.
