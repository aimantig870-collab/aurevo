# Aurevo — brand strategy & identity guidelines

> Concept stage. No operating café. Name availability is **not** trademark-cleared.

## 1. Name selection
| Name | Score | Verdict | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aurevo** | 9/10 | Chosen | *aurum* (gold) + the open vowels of *au revoir*. 3 syllables, no hard consonant clusters, works across languages. Web search: no café/coffee brand with this exact name. |

| Lumeroast | 4 | Rejected | Descriptive/generic; weak for licensing. |

**Before any commercial use:** official trademark search + counsel in each target country (classes 30, 35, 43), domain and handle checks.

## 2. Strategy
- **Positioning:** accessible-luxury specialty café; world-pantry flavours on a rigorous coffee base; architecture and brand built around the *golden hour*.
- **Philosophy:** slow down, glow up.
- **Mission:** make the quiet part of the day feel special, with drinks that surprise and standards that travel.
- **Vision:** a recognisable international café brand grown carefully — one proven location first, partners and licences only after operations are validated.
- **Personality:** warm · assured · curious · unhurried · generous · precise.
- **Values:** Slow is a skill · Warm, not loud · Honest origins · Crossroads flavour · Built to travel.
- **Slogan:** *Slow down. Glow up.* · **Tagline:** *Coffee for the golden hour.*
- **Differentiation:** (1) 14 original signature drinks, (2) a single ownable visual idea (sun + cup), (3) documented, franchise-ready standards, (4) radical honesty about what's tested.
- **Competitive position:** vs global chains (more distinctive, warmer) · vs third-wave cafés (same craft, friendlier menu) · vs trend-led dessert/tea brands (real espresso technique).
- **Voice:** short sentences, sensory words, light poetry, no superlatives we cannot prove ("proposed", "we'd pour"). Never: "best", "#1", "award-winning", "sold out" unless true.

## 3. Audience personas (fictional planning archetypes — not research)
1. **Layla, 29, creative professional** — wants a third place that looks as good as it tastes. *Golden Hour Latte, Rose Cloud Cold Brew.*
2. **Daniel, 38, hybrid worker & parent** — a dependable flat white and a calm hour. *Flat White, Silk Road Cappuccino.*
3. **Mei, 22, student & creator** — playful flavours, photogenic, fair prices. *Ube Cloud Latte, Midnight Sesame.*
4. **Omar, 45, prospective café partner** — wants a distinctive concept with honest economics.

## 4. Story
Every café has a golden hour — the stretch when sun slants low and everyone slows down. Aurevo is a café designed entirely around that moment: a menu like a long afternoon, a ritual that rewards patience, a brand recognisable by its glow.

## 5. Visual identity
**Logo system** (all vector, text-free paths — `public/art/logo/`):
- **Primary:** symbol + AUREVO logotype (horizontal). Light/dark variants.
- **Secondary:** stacked symbol, logotype, descriptor "GOLDEN HOUR COFFEE HOUSE".
- **Symbol:** a sun rising from a cup's rim inside a ring.
- **Monogram:** monoline "A" with a sun dot.
- **Logotype:** custom monoline geometric letters; the "O" is the sun resting on the horizon.
- Clear space = height of the "A"; minimum size 28 px / 10 mm (symbol 16 px / 6 mm). No stretching, shadows or off-palette colours.

**Typography:** Instrument Serif (display; regular + italic) · Hanken Grotesk (body/UI, 400–700). Eyebrows 12 px, +22% tracking, uppercase.
**Pattern:** "Sunrise tile" (arc + horizon + three rays). **Icons:** 3 px line, round caps (cup, bean, sun, leaf, croissant, pin, bag, clock).
**Applications (mockups in `public/art/mockups/`):** hot cup, cold cup, coffee bag, paper bag, pastry box, napkin, uniform, menu, business cards, signage, product label. These are flat identity mockups, **not print-ready dielines**.
