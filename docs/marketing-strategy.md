# Aurevo — marketing strategy

**Guardrails:** no purchased followers/engagement, no fabricated reviews, sales or tasting results, no "best/#1/sold-out" claims. While Aurevo has no café, all content is framed as *concept, development and behind-the-scenes*. Anything shown as a drink must be labelled "concept" until it has been physically tested and photographed.

## 1. Objectives (first 90 days — targets are goals, not forecasts)
Build an owned audience (email + social) · validate flavour interest (polls, saves, comments) · recruit 10–20 partner/supplier conversations · collect first-hand tasting feedback during bench tests.

## 2. Platform roles
| Platform | Role | Format | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Brand home & community | Reels, carousels, Stories polls | 4–5 posts/wk + daily Stories |
| TikTok | Discovery | Recipe-development process, flavour explainers, 15–30 s | 3–4/wk |
| Pinterest | Evergreen search traffic | Vertical pins of drinks, palette, café design → website | 5–10 pins/wk |
| Facebook | Partners & local community, events | Link posts, groups, longer stories | 2–3/wk |

## 3. Content pillars
1. **Golden Hour** (30%) — light, ritual, slow moments, café atmosphere.
2. **The Palette** (30%) — one ingredient per post: saffron, pandan, miso, tamarind, ube; where it comes from, how it's used.
3. **Behind the Brew** (20%) — recipe development, bench tests, honest failures, supplier visits.
4. **Design & Brand** (10%) — logo story, packaging, café design process.
5. **Partners** (10%) — partnership concept, franchise-readiness explainers (never earnings claims).

## 4. Campaigns
- **Brand awareness — "Thirty Minutes":** a series of 30-second golden-hour scenes; CTA: join the list.
- **Product launch (when a real pilot opens) — "First Light Week":** staged reveal of 3 signatures/week; tasting-event RSVP list.
- **Promotional (post-opening):** "Golden Hour Hours" 4–6 pm pairing offers (drink + bakery); loyalty stamp card with real, tracked redemption.
- **Partner acquisition:** "Bring the Palette to your café" LinkedIn/IG outreach with a signature-drink licensing one-pager.

## 5. Visual content guidelines
Warm light, brand palette only, one hero subject, generous negative space, serif headlines (Instrument Serif), sentence-case captions, logo bottom-left at ≥ 8% width, "concept" tag on non-real products. Reels: 3-second hook (pour/steam), text on-screen, captions on, 9:16.

## 6. Advertising concepts
1. **"Slow down. Glow up."** — time-lapse of sun crossing a cup.
2. **"Pantry Passport"** — each drink as a stamp from its ingredient's region.
3. **"Light Menu"** — the menu board changes colour with time of day (digital OOH).
4. **"Taste test, honestly"** — real tasters score proposed recipes; show the weak scores too.

## 7. Influencer collaboration
Micro-creators (5–50k) in coffee, food, design and travel; gifted tastings only after recipes are tested; contractual disclosure (#ad/#gifted) per local rules; no scripted superlatives; track with unique links/codes; pay fairly. Start with 10 outreach messages/week.

## 8. Customer acquisition
Owned: email waitlist (website forms), referral. Earned: creators, press with the design/story, local food media. Paid (small tests, $10–20/day): Reels to lookalike of coffee/food interests; measure cost per waitlist signup. SEO: product pages already structured (title/description/sitemap); add blog "ingredient guides".

## 9. Growth strategy
Phase 1 audience → Phase 2 pilot café/pop-up (real data) → Phase 3 partner cafés/licensed drinks → Phase 4 franchise only after readiness gates (see `franchise-framework.md`).

## 10. KPIs (measure real numbers only)
Waitlist signups · saves/shares per post · profile→site CTR · partnership/franchise inquiry count (from the admin inbox) · cost per signup · tasting-panel scores.
