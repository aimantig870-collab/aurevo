# Aurevo — future franchise operating framework

> **Status: design only.** Aurevo is **not** an available or validated franchise. No offer may be made until the readiness gates at the bottom are met and local counsel has produced compliant disclosure documents.

## 1. Franchise presentation (structure)
Brand story · concept & formats · unit economics (**from the pilot's real data**) · support provided · investment categories · franchisee profile · process & timeline · legal disclosure documents.

## 2. Brand usage guidelines
Use only approved logo files; honour clear space/minimum size; palette and fonts per `/brand`; no co-branding without approval; approved social-handle conventions; all claims must be truthful ("proposed" → "approved" only with QA sign-off).

## 3. Store design standards
Façade: arches or equivalent, charcoal fascia with logotype, 2700 K lighting. Interior: oak/plaster/terracotta/brass palette; counter faces guests; open retail shelving; photography corner. Formats: flagship 80–120 m², kiosk 15–30 m², shop-in-shop licence. Layout approval by Aurevo design team before construction.

## 4. Menu standards
Core menu (espresso, milk, cold) mandatory; 8 of 14 signatures mandatory, remainder optional; local-market specials allowed with approval; allergen and calorie labelling per jurisdiction; recipe cards with quantities, temperature, glassware, garnish and photo reference.

## 5. Product preparation standards
Espresso 18 g in / 36 g out / 25–30 s (tune per bean); milk 60–65 °C; syrups made to written batch specs with labelled dates (shelf-life set by food-safety testing); station set-up diagrams; waste logging.

## 6. Staff training framework
Week 1 hospitality & hygiene · Week 2 espresso & milk · Week 3 signatures & garnish · Week 4 service, POS, closing. Certification checklist per barista; refresher every 6 months.

## 7. Quality control
Monthly mystery-guest score (≥ 90%) · quarterly recipe-compliance tasting · temperature and extraction logs · supplier traceability · customer-complaint SLA 48 h · corrective-action plan for failures.

## 8. Marketing guidelines
Central brand assets; local-store marketing plan approved by HQ; social posting rules (no unverifiable claims; disclose gifted content); contribution to a marketing fund (rate to be defined).

## 9. Operational checklist (daily)
Open: calibrate grinder, taste espresso, check fridge temps, stock syrups with dates · Service: ticket-time targets, cleanliness walk · Close: purge/backflush, waste log, cash reconcile, orders · Weekly: deep-clean, inventory count · Monthly: P&L review, QC score.

## 10. Franchise inquiry process
1. Website form (`/franchise`, requires concept-stage acknowledgement) → admin inbox.
2. Qualification call (experience, capital, location, market). 3. Information pack (once legally permitted). 4. Disclosure period + legal review. 5. Site approval. 6. Agreement, training, fit-out, pre-opening audit. 7. Opening + 90-day support.
Inquiry statuses in the admin: new → in review → contacted → closed (or spam).

## Readiness gates (all required before "franchise available")
- [ ] Pilot café operated by Aurevo with real sales data
- [ ] Recipes bench-tested; costs verified with supplier quotes
- [ ] Trademarks searched and registered in target countries
- [ ] Franchise disclosure documents/agreements by qualified counsel per country
- [ ] Operations manual and training validated in the pilot
- [ ] Audited unit economics
